# ResetPath User Manual

ResetPath is designed to be quiet and personal. There are no leaderboards,
social feeds, rankings, or public profiles. Everything described here stays on
your device unless you choose to export it.

> It is not medical care or a replacement for counselling, a doctor, or
> emergency support.

## 1. Install and begin

1. Download the ARM64 APK from the repository release page.
2. Open the file named `resetpath-arm64-v8a.apk`.
3. If Android asks, allow the browser or file manager to install apps from that
   source.
4. Open ResetPath and read the safety notice.
5. Use the settings icon to switch between light and dark themes, add a trusted
   contact, or review data controls.

The first screen is **Today**. It offers three gentle choices: reset, plan a
small step, or write.

## 2. Use Reset now when you are spiralling

Tap **I’m spiralling** or **Reset now**. Move through the five short screens at
your own speed:

1. Breathe with the expanding visual.
2. Notice five things you can see.
3. Put the frightening thought into words.
4. Separate what is known now from a prediction about the future.
5. Pick one ten-minute action that you control.

You can leave at any time. Finishing the flow is not a score and does not mean
you have to feel better immediately.

## 3. Plan today’s three tasks

Open **Tasks** or tap **Small step**. Add a task that is small enough to begin,
such as “one array problem,” “twenty minutes of DBMS,” or “create one project
function.” Choose a track:

- DSA
- Core CS
- Project development
- Applications

When you complete a task, mark it done. ResetPath creates a private evidence
record; it does not compare you with anyone else.

## 4. Write in the private diary

Open **Journal** and write normally, like a diary or blank notebook. A page can
be saved, reopened, edited, exported with your other data, or removed through
the app’s data controls. You do not need to answer prompts or fill boxes.

Diary pages are not used to train the model. By default they are not analysed
at all.

## 5. Use the optional guided reflection

The guided reflection is separate from the diary. It asks one gentle question
at a time so you can unpack a difficult thought and choose one realistic action.

You may optionally enable **Use relevant private diary pages for this one
response**. When enabled, ResetPath finds up to three matching excerpts on your
phone only, for that reflection only. It shows the selected context before you
continue. It does not send the text to a server, permanently profile you, or
train the local model.

The offline tone hint may say a message sounds like fear, sadness, anger, joy,
love, or surprise. Treat it as a fallible writing aid: choose your own emotion
if it does not fit.

## 6. Review evidence and progress

Open **Evidence** to see completed tasks and private progress charts. The goal
is to collect counter-evidence to thoughts such as “I never do anything,” not
to build a streak or compete with others.

The **Body & mood context** area can import a wearable export in CSV or JSON
format and store optional mood or energy check-ins. These are descriptive notes,
not a stress score, diagnosis, or health recommendation.

## 7. Meditation, voice, haptics, and appearance

- **Meditation:** choose a short two-, five-, or ten-minute visual session.
- **Voice typing:** tap your phone keyboard’s microphone while focused on a
  writing field. ResetPath receives the resulting text but does not record
  audio. Offline behaviour depends on your keyboard settings.
- **Haptics:** supported phones give a subtle vibration for selected actions.
- **Theme:** use Settings to choose light or dark mode; the choice is saved
  locally.

## 8. Get human help

Open **Help** to contact a trusted person, use campus-counsellor details you
add, or open Tele-MANAS information. If you may act on thoughts of self-harm or
cannot remain safe, seek immediate human help or local emergency services.
ResetPath cannot decide whether you are safe.

## 9. Export or delete your data

In Settings, use **Export** to create a local ZIP copy of the supported app
data. Use **Delete all data** only when you are ready to permanently remove
local ResetPath records. Export first if you want a backup.

ResetPath has no account and no cloud sync in this version. Reinstalling the
app can remove locally stored information, so export anything important before
uninstalling.

## 10. Navigation and troubleshooting

- Use the app back button to return to the previous ResetPath screen.
- On Android, the system back action should return to the previous app page
  before it exits the app.
- If a downloaded APK will not install, confirm that it matches your phone’s
  CPU type, permit installation from that source, and ensure you have enough
  free storage.
- If dictation is missing, enable the microphone in your keyboard app, not in
  ResetPath.
- If you find a bug, include your Android version, phone model, ResetPath
  version, and exact steps to reproduce it in a GitHub issue.

See [Limitations and release readiness](12_LIMITATIONS.md) before relying on a
feature that is marked as planned.
