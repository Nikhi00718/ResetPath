# ResetPath User Manual

ResetPath is quiet and personal. There are no feeds, rankings, or streaks you
can lose. Everything stays on your phone unless you save a backup.

> It is not medical care or a replacement for counselling, a doctor, or
> emergency support.

## Install

1. Copy `resetpath.apk` to your phone and open it.
2. If Android asks, allow your file manager or browser to install apps.
3. Open ResetPath. If you are upgrading from 0.1 or 0.2, your tasks and diary
   pages are brought over automatically.
4. When you first play a sound, allow notifications. Android needs this to keep
   sound playing with the screen off.

## Today

- **Mood**: tap one face. You can change it any time that day.
- **Tasks**: type a task, pick its track chip, and press Enter. Tap the circle
  to complete it. Tap the ⋯ button to edit it, move it to tomorrow, or delete
  it. If earlier days have unfinished tasks, one button moves them all to today.
- **Habits**: tap a tile to count it. Long-press to undo one count. Tap
  **Edit** to add a habit (with an emoji and times per day) or remove one.
- **Quick actions** jump to Focus, Sounds, breathing, or a new journal page.

## Focus

1. Choose the focus length and break length.
2. Pick a sound for focus, or Silence.
3. Optionally pick one of today's open tasks.
4. Tap **Start**. The ring around the orb fills as time passes. The screen
   stays on while the timer runs.

When a block ends, you get a chime, a vibration, and a notification. The break
is ready to start. **Skip** ends the current block early and still records the
minutes you spent. A running timer appears in the bar above the tabs on every
screen.

## Sounds

- Tap a sound to turn it on or off. Drag its slider to mix it.
- **Mixes** are presets: Deep focus, Rainy study, Sleep, Forest night, and
  Fan + white.
- **Sleep timer** fades the sound out over the last minute and then stops it.
- The mini player above the tabs pauses or stops the sound from any screen.
- Pressing Android back on the Today screen sends the app to the background
  without stopping the sound.

## Calm

- **Guided breathing**: follow the orb. It grows as you breathe in and shrinks
  as you breathe out. The phone taps gently when the phase changes. Use Calm
  4·6 for everyday stress, Box before an exam or interview, and 4·7·8 at bedtime.
- **2-minute reset**: look around, write the thought, read a steadier view,
  and pick one small next step. Then add that step to today or start a
  10-minute focus on it.
- **Journal**: private pages with optional writing prompts. Tap a page to edit
  or delete it.

## You

- Weekly focus minutes, tasks done, and active days.
- A five-week habit heatmap, a two-week mood strip, and tasks done by track
  over 30 days.
- Settings: your name, dark or light theme, and motion (Full, Low, or Off; Low
  and Off save battery).
- Trusted person: name and phone. A call button appears on the Calm screen.
- **Save backup** and **Restore backup** before you change phones or uninstall.
- **Delete everything** clears all data on this phone.
